## 6. Published research — Forward Deployed Engineer hiring study

Page: https://sameer-goel.com/forward-deployed-engineer/

A reproducible job-market measurement pipeline, not an opinion piece. This is arguably the
strongest single credential in this document for FDE and data-oriented roles, because the
methodology is auditable.

**What was built**

- Collected **4,241** Forward Deployed Engineer and customer-embedded job postings from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, aggregators, and RSS feeds
- Classified on **body text rather than job title**; demonstrated that title-only filtering misses 2,521 postings and 233 companies
- Independently re-verified against **515 live postings** pulled same-day from employer APIs
- **Published the 12 terms where the two samples disagree by more than 15 points** instead of suppressing them
- Fully reproducible: `node .research/verify-keywords-live.mjs`; every published percentage is generated from JSON, never hand-typed
- Page itself is generated by `build.mjs` from measurement outputs, so the prose cannot drift from the data

**Resume line candidates**

> Built and published a reproducible job-market analysis across 4,241 job postings with same-day API re-verification, disclosing sampling contradictions rather than smoothing them.

> Designed a text-classification pipeline that measured 4,241 job postings, proving title-based filtering omits 2,521 postings and 233 companies; results re-verified against 515 live API postings and published with contradictions intact.
