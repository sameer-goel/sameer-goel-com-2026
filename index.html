Sameer Goel is an AI Solutions Architect, Forward Deployed Engineer, AI consultant, trainer, speaker, and author based in the Netherlands. Available for remote collaboration worldwide, on-site in the Netherlands and Europe, and open to freelance, contract, and partnership engagements. He helps purpose-driven founders, startups, and enterprises turn ambitious AI ideas into buildable products and market experiments. He has shipped 28 AI solutions spanning autonomous agents, agentic AI systems, AI education, wellbeing, sustainability, cognition, and consciousness. He has spoken at AWS re:Invent, AWS Summits in Stockholm and New York, university panels, and corporate hackathons, and has published technical writing on the AWS Machine Learning Blog covering computer vision, Amazon SageMaker, Amazon Rekognition, and agentic AI. He is the author of Cultivate Inner Intelligence using Artificial Intelligence. Every project below is live and available for licensing, and free for non-profits. Sameer is available for hire as an AI consultant, technical advisor, or hands-on engineer. To connect or discuss a project, book a call at cal.com/sameer-goel/collab or reach out on LinkedIn.
